## Music

`../audio/epilogue-music.mp3` (72 s) is a two-part epilogue score. It starts
immediately at full volume - no silent lead-in:

1. `0:00 - 0:41` - Gustav Holst, "The Planets, Op. 32 - I. Mars, the Bringer
   of War" (the loud march section, from 0:56 of the source recording), cut
   from the project-supplied recording. The composition is in the public
   domain; the recording was supplied with the project and its original
   source/recording licence was not included with the file.
2. `0:41 - 1:12` - Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky, "1812 Overture" (final brass
   section), from the United States Marine Band recording on Wikimedia Commons
   (public domain):
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1812_Overture_-_United_States_Marine_Band.opus
   Crossfaded into the Holst. The track loops on the end card and stops only
   when the player clicks PLAY AGAIN.

The 1812 finale is used as the triumphant resolution of the epilogue. Replace
both excerpts with verified recordings before commercial distribution.
