How to play

Eight sessions. Ten years. One lesson.

  1. Read the notes on the left. They say what is going wrong and where to fix it. Click one to go there.
  2. Open the planning session (top right). Every measure says what it costs, how long it takes and what it does. Order what you think the town needs, then Give the orders.
  3. Fifteen months pass. Post arrives as it goes. You can skip to the end.
  4. Read the recap in the middle: what changed, and which of your orders it followed. Then plan again. Eight sessions and ten years are gone.

Three things nobody will tell you. How worn the machines are (have them checked), how people really feel (only a poll, with error in it), and what happens next (the dashed line is a guess, not a promise).
